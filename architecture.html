02 / COMPONENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Distinct services.
Connected carefully.

Authentication and operations stay separate from market analysis and simulated execution.

NODE.JS / EXPRESSGATEWAY

Account & session boundary

Upstox OAuth, server-side HttpOnly sessions, CSRF enforcement, account ownership checks, dashboard views and authenticated paper-session controls.

PYTHON / FASTAPIENGINE

Decision and risk pipeline

Market-data intake, strategy evaluation, filter chains, strike selection and simulated trade lifecycle. The gateway does not expose public live orders.

REDIS + MONGODBSTATE

Operational state and history

Redis coordinates session commands, risk locks and short-lived state. MongoDB stores trades, signals and history used by analytics and research.

DUCKDB + PARQUETREPLAY

Market capture and simulation

Tick/depth recordings and manifests support event-clock replay and a depth-aware fill simulator. Complete-session fidelity remains an explicit validation task.
