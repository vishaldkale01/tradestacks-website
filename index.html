01 / THE PLATFORM

Built to test ideas.
Not to promise outcomes.

Every component is designed to make an automated trading decision inspectable, reproducible, and measurable.

⌁
01 / ANALYZE

Market intelligence

Rule-based candidate signals and market context using VWAP, momentum, multi-timeframe trends and layered filters.

◇
02 / PROTECT

Risk-first execution

Designed around position management, stops, cooldowns and session-level loss controls—not unconstrained automation.

↺
03 / REPLAY

Historical market replay

Recorded market data can be replayed to investigate how strategies and simulated execution behave under past conditions.

▥
04 / EXPLAIN

Trade-level diagnostics

Capture signals, entries, exits and estimated trading costs to understand why a trading session gained or lost.

02 / OUR APPROACH

Observe.
Simulate.
Improve.

TradeStack separates research and simulation from real-money execution. The goal is a more trustworthy engineering process, not a guaranteed profit curve.

01

Listen to the market

Process real-time index and option data into structured signals.

02

Check the risk

Evaluate execution conditions and predefined session limits.

03

Paper trade and record

Simulate positions and preserve useful decision evidence.

04

Replay and investigate

Audit outcomes, costs, and strategy behavior before changing rules.
